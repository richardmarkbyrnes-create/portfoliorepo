context & opportunity

Miro Insights, an acquired AI platform, aggregated qualitative customer data across tools like Gong, Salesforce, and Zendesk to surface actionable voice-of-customer insights tied directly to revenue opportunities and the features they requested.


PROBLEM SPACE

Disconnection from
where the work happened.

  • Before rich customer feedback sat in siloed tools — Zendesk, Gong, Jira, etc.. Insights provided a solution for this but was disconnected from where the real synthesis that happened collaboratively on the canvas, in roadmapping, backlog grooming and sprint planning across product, design, research and even go to market teams.

The opportunity

A strategic pillar for our products growth we saw an opportunity to leverage Miro's canvas and 100+ million user base to scale adoption of Miro Insights. The main issue was that we needed to understand how users actually used customer insights in their existing workflows in Miro today.

Paired with Brittney to understand this space deeper.

Conducted 16 interviews and synthesis observation across three contrasting personas: Researchers, Product Managers, and Sales to give us a deeper understanding of how they used insights in their Miro workflows today.

What we discovered

Core breakdown points
during insight synthesis

Processes were messy. Standard outputs lacked metadata, blocking collaborators from verifying or digging deeper into the data themselves devaluing the work the creators put in.

Insights lost their customer tier, source transcript, and revenue weight once on the canvas.
Static board data decayed overnight, decoupling ongoing team decisions from evolving customer feedback.

OUR 3 CORE PERSONAS

They worked in distinctly different ways but still grounded on the same data.

Product managers cared most about the volume of a specific feature mentioned. Researchers care more about holistic themes and patterns while Sales team cared more about using company data and requests to roadmap their pipelines.

WE GOT TO EXPLORING

We knew how they worked, we recognized the issues and gaps in their existing workflows and we saw room to improve.

We ideated and brainstormed potential solutions to that we felt were a viable first step solution to address this problem space.

DESIGN pillars

1. Trust behind the data was a huge barrier for adoption

2. Insights gain value only when they can be seamlessly used in multiplayer environments.

3. Data should be bi directional and synced to avoid data decay and outdated artifacts left on boards.

we landed on 3 potentials routes (3 horizontal rows here)

1. Concept of adding an invisible data layer to already existing features (sticky notes, docs, screenshots) and when selected users can triggers Miro AI to learn more about this specific insight.

2. A brand new insights format that when data was added to inside the format container it automatically sync to our insights database.

3. Structured insight cards components and a new app experience to find and discover feedback straight from the canvas. Winner winner chicken dinner.

WHAT WE BUILT

Turning scattered insight data into structured, multiplayer components on the Miro canvas.

Insight cards bring the source of truth onto the canvas. They restructure themselves depending on where you drop them — compact inside a table, spanning a kanban column, opening out to the full customer quote when they stand alone — so they fit whatever structure a team already works in. Either way the card links back to the original feedback, so anyone can trace a workshop conclusion back to something a customer actually said.

the results

87% adoption rate from our existing user base from our standalone product experience. This drove adoption of Miro and also increased our monthly active usage metrics of our platform.

THE STRUGGLES

Despite many successes with this project we struggled with a major discoverability problem. Unless users were told to look they weren't aware of the product offering which hurt our adoption rate going forward.

We addessed this later with contextual prompts triggered during user workflows to prompt them to use Insights and also deeply intergated into Miro's AI chat and MCP experiences. But that's a story for another day.
